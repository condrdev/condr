# Panes Search Their Scrollback and Show a Scrollbar Off the Bottom

Status: accepted 2026-11-26. Takes up the scrollback search ADR 0051 left for later.

An Agent's output runs long, and finding an error or a path in it means scrolling until it shows. The history lives only in the Pane's VT on the Server, 50 MB of it (ADR 0051), so a search has to run there. Nothing on screen says how deep that history is or where the viewport sits in it either: a view frame carries `display_offset`, the rows below the viewport, and nothing about the rows above it.

libghostty-vt at the pinned commit already has both halves. Its search (`search.h`) covers the active area and scrollback of the primary and alternate screens, keeps its results in step with output, screen switches, resize, reflow and pruning, and is driven in bounded steps: `feed` reads the terminal, `tick` works on what was fed and never touches it. It matches bytes exactly except ASCII letters, which compare without case; it has no regular expressions. Its viewport scrolls to an absolute row (`GHOSTTY_SCROLL_VIEWPORT_ROW`). Condr binds neither yet. Ghostty, VS Code's terminal and iTerm2 float their find bar over the terminal's top right corner.

## The scrollbar

**Frames say how much history there is.** Full and delta frames carry `history_rows`, the scrollback rows above the active area, beside `display_offset`; with the Pane's row count the Client knows the content's height and where the viewport sits in it. `TerminalScroll` gains `Row`, the viewport's top counted from the top of the scrollback, which the VT applies as an absolute row. A row from the top holds still while output arrives under a viewport that is off the bottom, as Ghostty's viewport does, so a drag does not wander.

**The GUI draws Kit's `Scrollbar` over the Pane's right edge**, through a `ScrollbarHandle` for the terminal: its content is the history and the screen in rows times the line height, its offset the viewport's top, and setting the offset sends `Row`. It takes no column; the grid keeps its size. Kit's track is 16 px wide and its thumb 6 to 8 px, more than the 5 px inset and the cells' remainder beside the grid, so while it shows it lies over part of the last column, and a press within the last 16 px goes to it, as VS Code's terminal scrollbar does. A gutter of its own was declined below. At the bottom, where a Pane is nearly always, the GUI draws no bar until the pointer is over the Pane's last 16 px or a drag holds the bar; then, off the bottom, or while the Pane has a search, Kit's bar shows in `Always` mode. A Pane without history has none. Kit's own `Hover` mode was tried and declined: Kit shows its bar for a moment whenever the offset changes or its state is new, which at the bottom every line of output, every reflow and every Pane shown again would do.

## The search

**A Pane has one search, as it has one scroll position** (ADR 0036). The needle lives on the Server beside the Pane's VT; the last Client to set it sets it for all, and it stays when a Client leaves, as the selection does (ADR 0008). A Snapshot never holds it (ADR 0005). `TerminalCommand` gains `Search`, the needle, an empty one ending the search, and `SearchStep`, older or newer.

**The Server runs it in steps.** A Pane's libghostty-vt search is made with its first needle and freed when the search ends or the Pane does. `condr-ghostty-vt` keeps it on the terminal, which frees it first, so every call is serialized by the VT's lock without a lock of its own. Each frame taken moves it on: one `feed`, then `tick` until the library asks for the next feed or 3 ms are spent, so output and input wait at most that long. While it has not caught up, the Pane's monitor wakes every 16 ms to take the next step, and a step that found more is a frame of its own; once caught up, the frame that output brings feeds it, so the highlights match the frame. Ghostty ticks off its terminal's lock on a thread of its own; that waits until a search of a full scrollback is measured holding up output. A step selects the next match, older or newer, wrapping at the ends, and the library scrolls the viewport to it only when it is off screen; that moves every Client's view, as any scroll does (ADR 0036).

**Frames carry what the Client draws.** Full and delta frames carry the Pane's search: the needle, how many matches it has found, the selected one's place counted from the newest, whether it has caught up, and the matches and the selected match that fall in the viewport, clipped to it as the selection is (ADR 0008). A delta carries it only when it changed. It rides the visual stream (ADR 0004); the highlights are painted under the text like the selection and invalidate no shaping cache.

**The find bar floats at the Pane's top right**, over the right end of its top rows, so opening or closing it never resizes the terminal or makes its program redraw. It holds the field, the count as "3/17", older and newer buttons, and a close button; once the search has caught up with nothing found, the count reads "No results". `find` (Cmd+F on macOS, Ctrl+Shift+F elsewhere; rebindable, ADR 0063) opens it on the focused Pane with the keyboard in its field, or selects the field's text when it is already open. It is also the first item of the Pane menu, in a group of its own above Split Right, and the Command Palette lists it as it lists every window shortcut (ADR 0064). The needle follows each keystroke, Enter steps older, which is up from the prompt, Shift+Enter newer, and Escape or the close button ends the search and gives the keyboard back to the terminal. Since the search is the Pane's, the bar shows in every window that shows the Pane while it has a needle. Matches take colours of their own, as herdr's copy-mode search gives them: every match the scheme's text colour on its background raised a fifth of the way toward that text, and the selected one, bold, the interface's accent (the scheme's ANSI blue) under the text Kit's theme chose for it. All of it comes from the colour scheme, so it follows the scheme and the light or dark mode; the Highlight theme has no say, since Kit's highlight theme has no field for find matches and the bundled code themes rarely set one. A tint of the scheme's yellow was tried first and turned muddy brown in light schemes, whose yellow is made for text. The count is "3/17" with a match selected and the number found without one, with a "+" while the search has not caught up and the number may still grow.

An older Server ignores `Search`, `SearchStep` and `Row`, as a peer ignores an unknown oneof member (ADR 0028), and sends no `history_rows`: the GUI draws no scrollbar and its bar never shows a count.

## Considered

- **A bar across the Pane's top**, as Kit's editor search in the Preview Tab is. Declined: it takes a row or two from the terminal, so every open and close resizes the PTY and the Agent redraws.
- **A search per Client.** Declined: a step scrolls the one viewport every Client shares (ADR 0036), so each Client's own needle would still move the others.
- **Regular expressions, case-sensitive and whole-word matching.** Declined: the library offers none of them. Whoever needs them has `condr pane read --lines N | grep`, which is also why the CLI gets no search command.
- **A scrollbar always shown when there is history**, as ADR 0076 does for the sidebar's lists. Declined: nearly every Pane has history, and at the bottom the bar says nothing.
- **A scrollbar column of its own**, so the bar never covers text. Declined: it takes a column from every Pane's grid, all the time, for a bar shown only off the bottom.
- **A thinner bar that fits the inset.** Declined: a 3 px thumb is hard to grab.
- **Tinting matches the way the Preview Tab's editor search does**, with Kit's selection colour. Declined: the selected match would look like a mouse selection.

## Not in this decision

Left for later, each with its trigger: marks for the matches along the scrollbar, which need every match's row and, for a common needle in 50 MB of history, many thousands of them after each feed, when people ask where in the history the matches are; search on the Companion, which decodes and ignores the new fields, when people search from the phone.

## Delivery

1. **Scrollbar.** `condr-ghostty-vt` binds the row scroll; frames carry `history_rows`; `TerminalScroll::Row`; the GUI's `ScrollbarHandle` and Kit `Scrollbar`.
2. **Search.** `script/update-libghostty-vt.sh` adds `search.h`'s functions and `ghostty_terminal_point_from_grid_ref` to its allowlist at the same pin, with the safe layer over them; the Server's search, the monitor's steps, the commands and the frames' search; the GUI's find bar, highlights, `find` shortcut and Pane menu item, and their `en` and `zh-CN` text.
3. **Docs.** AGENTS.md, the website's Workspaces page, shortcut page and configuration reference in both languages, and the roadmap without this item.

## Tests

`condr-ghostty-vt`: a needle found in scrollback and on the alternate screen, the count newest first, a step scrolling to an off-screen match, the matches on the viewport, the row scroll. `condr-core`: a view carrying the search, a step moving the viewport, output arriving during a search raising the count, an empty needle ending it; a search that has not caught up waking the monitor, a step that found more taking a frame of its own, a delta without the search when it did not change; `history_rows`, the search, `Search`, `SearchStep` and `Row` round-tripping, a Bootstrap's view among them. GUI: the scrollbar handle's rows and pixels, and a drag holding the thumb until the viewport moves; dragging the thumb in a real window moving the Server's viewport; `find`, from its shortcut and from the Pane menu, opening the bar with the keyboard in its field and never resizing the Pane; typing searching without scrolling; Enter stepping to a match off screen; Escape ending the search and focusing the terminal; frames that change only the search reshaping no cell outside the matches; the cells' marks, the selected match over the others. By hand on Windows, as AGENTS.md asks: searching a Pane while Codex streams, with interaction and frame rate observed.
